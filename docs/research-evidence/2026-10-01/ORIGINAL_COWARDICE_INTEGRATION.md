The client supplies no target, damage, killer, health or direct-versus-hurt choice. [Parser](../../../server/protocol.ts) and [authority](../../../server/match-room.ts) validate the envelope, current peer and receipt generation. Authority derives the operation from its current map and typing state, advances its normal source world, and applies literal1000 damage when required.

Cowardice has a separate event cause rather than a forged bullet or explosion. Its nullable actor identity controls directional hurt lookup; the literal result label remains `Cowardice`. Direct death emits no damaged event or hurtme sample. Hurt uses the recovered local sample and signed health/state epoch. Lethal damage waits for the killed receipt before client departure. Authority's existing kill path owns exactly one death, persistent source weapon drop, reload cancellation and frozen death pose. The usual explicit leave then releases room ownership. Remote corpse presentation continues after the actor leaves the live room and ends through the existing source fade adapter.

The original literal killer name can resolve another actor named Cowardice and award it30 points. Director equality is case-insensitive for ASCII; the shared helper covers all512 case combinations without inventing extended-character equivalences; an own non-talk MUS message is excluded, so a player named Cowardice does not award itself points. Both source MUS handlers display the remote feedback `Cowardice makes VICTIM russian history.`. Source deaths now put this recovered phrase in ordinary feedback, with the actual killer name for other source deaths; own death produces no remote feedback. Cowardice does not invent a weapon hitmarker, projectile effect or flinch. A later [source fadeaway audit](ORIGINAL_DAMAGE_LIFECYCLE_GAP_AUDIT.md) corrects the earlier remote-sound exclusion: remote corpse birth unconditionally plays positional hurt, while direct local Barrack death still omits hurtme.

## Limits and acceptance

DOM Escape/key-repeat and browser capture behavior are platform adapters. Native getplayer can briefly resolve dead or detached actors retained in its local list; this authority preserves its current-peer/live-actor eligibility. Own-name lookup has source priority. Global authority scoring does not establish duplicate-name observer behavior. Source requests are serialized until response; native key dispatch within Director frames and original network/audio timing are unproved. Authority retains the existing nominal callback phase/epoch reconciliation and view-receipt limits. The high-health fixture proves the copy's actual browser/server route; no recovered ordinary map placement supplies a1100-health spawn. Extended-character name comparison and duplicate-name restrictions, corpse raster/timing and room-wide reset/server-disconnection limits remain unproved.

A [subsequent source audit](ORIGINAL_DEPARTED_OWNER_EXPLOSIVES_AUDIT.md) identifies a concrete remaining G05/G06 discrepancy: source explosives and active blasts survive their owner’s departure on other clients, while current authority removes or suppresses them. That gap was outstanding at this Cowardice baseline; the [subsequent explosive integration](ORIGINAL_DEPARTED_OWNER_EXPLOSIVES_INTEGRATION.md) implements it.

This correction advances G05 without accepting the complete lifecycle requirement. Acceptance remains27/40, with all thirteen broader open points active.
