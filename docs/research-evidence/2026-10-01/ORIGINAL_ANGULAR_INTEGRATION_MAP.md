# Integration map for source angular rigid bodies

Bounded read-only engineering audit of the current copy. No production/test changes, GPU checks or original Director runtime were executed. This map identifies what a coherent angular implementation needs; it does not establish native mass properties, manifolds or rotational collision timing, and does not accept V10/G06 or the assisted-jump criterion.

## Source scope and invariants

Both editions' `ParentScript 14 - grenades.ls` create unpinned convex mass-one bodies with restitution .2/friction .5, restore recycled body rotation from model axisAngle, and zero angular velocity at launch (Woodland lines18,25,29). Contact handler lines53–54 multiplies **linear** velocity by .85; it does not prescribe angular damping. `ParentScript 10 - barrels.ls` lines6–9 lifts the original model 10 source units and creates an unpinned convex mass-one body with restitution .2/friction 1.

RPG is also source-proven unpinned: `ParentScript 15 - rockets.ls` lines19–20 creates a convex body then sets mass 100; lines23–27 restores recycled orientation; line33 zeroes angular velocity only at birth. Its actor lines41–44 resets **linear** velocity to launch speed 800 each callback; no later angular reset occurs. Its hit handler lines73–74 marks impact. Any angular extension must retain its launch sourceRotation as immutable launch metadata, separately from evolving body orientation.

`ParentScript 21 - worldphysics.ls` lines42–44 steps .05 seconds with 10 substeps. Existing nominal 30 Hz actor scheduling, gravity -400 source units, one-total-source-unit contact distance, masses, current source restitution/friction assignments, .85 grenade hit callback, gun/body/projectile ordering, fuse rules, and strict explosive-jump height acceptance must remain intact. Native coefficient combination and callback notification cadence remain separate unresolved questions; changing them or replacing full hulls with spheres is not an angular implementation.

## Contact points are missing from public results

`OriginalProjectileContact.position` explicitly means **body pivot at contact**, not surface point (`shared/originalExplosives.ts` lines45–47). `OriginalConvexSweepHit.position` likewise interpolates the moving pivot. `OriginalConvexContact` exports only fraction, normal, penetration depth and overlap state. Internal joint `Event` retains no witness positions. Using any of these pivot positions as an angular impulse location would silently invent the torque arm.

Geometry already contains useful intermediate witnesses: `originalConvexContact.closestTriangle` computes nearest surface points for convex/sphere distance, and `originalContactDistance.closestTriangle`, `closestSegments`, and feature candidate comparisons compute point pairs before discarding them into `{distance,normal}`. Fixed-triangle sweeps retain access to triangle vertices in `OriginalSceneGeometry.sweepConvex`. A bounded implementation can expose those mathematical witnesses and feature identifiers through the complete query path, but must additionally resolve overlap and face-face manifold selection. A single arbitrary support vertex for a flat face can introduce artificial torque; it is not a substitute for a justified manifold rule.

`OriginalPhysicsContactDiagnostic` currently records linear velocities before response, after response and after actor callbacks. Add angular velocities, COM/pivot poses, contact witnesses and impulses to meaningful diagnostics rather than inferring rotation from final positions. Existing source .85 callback applies after response to linear velocity only.

## Client/server/transport integration

- `shared/protocol.ts`: `OriginalBarrelSnapshot` is currently id/position/velocity; `ProjectileSnapshot` has position/velocity and immutable launch sourceRotation. Add finite validated orientation/angular state without conflating launch metadata, with explicit protocol negotiation if the contract changes.
- `src/network.ts`: barrel validation around 429–431 and projectile validation around 380–397 currently know no evolving angular state. Require complete finite orientation and angular fields under the negotiated source contract; clone every mutable vector/state during adoption and prediction. Existing sourceRotation/direction consistency checks must remain launch-only.
- `server/match-room.ts`: initial source projectile snapshot at 1551–1552, controlled joint world at 1678, uncontrolled joint world at 1739, standalone projectile advancement around 1826, snapshot field refresh around 1707/1762 and `cloneProjectile` around 2282 all need evolving angular state. Barrel snapshots are emitted from the same `OriginalBarrelWorld`. Movement validation around 1165 uses the current barrel hull, so restored/rotated hull geometry must reach it too. Room reset/retained-player behavior must not leak angular state across lives or rooms.
- `src/game.ts`: `updateOriginalPlayerCallback` around 3150 and replay reconciliation around 3233 step shared barrel physics. Solo projectile rendering around 3161 currently reapplies launch orientation after every callback. `reconcileNetworkProjectile` around 1950 constructs a fresh fixed-launch source state on every authority adoption; `renderNetworkProjectilePrediction` around 1981 shallow-clones it and orients from direction/sourceRotation. These paths must restore evolving orientation/angular velocity, clone it independently, and render its body orientation. Launch helpers and pending/adopted projectile replacement must retain the same original local mesh offset.
- `src/originalArena.ts` `syncBarrels` around 49–62 copies the imported full matrix and replaces only translation. Its angular version must retain imported mesh/shader/scale while converting world physics rotation back through the outer source-Z-up scene group. `barrelModelPivots` should continue reporting the true rendered pivot for comparison with physical COM-to-pivot reconstruction.

## Bounded implementation order and blockers

1. Define local hull, physical COM, pivot, orientation and linear/angular velocity conventions explicitly. Generic Havok Primer p 27 distinguishes model pivot from COM and says rotation uses COM; p 29 establishes off-center impulse torque. It does not prove Xtra's imported convex mass distribution, body.position convention or inertia construction. For a documented adapter, rotating with local COM offset `c` requires rendered pivot `COM - rotation*c`, not rotation about a bottom/resource pivot.
2. Add independent mass-property and point-impulse mathematical checks before changing runtime: symmetric center hit gives zero angular response, off-center impulse signs/units, body-frame/world inertia transformation, angular-momentum conservation, and mass-one versus mass 100. Native COM/inertia/mass-update rules remain a primary-document blocker to equivalence, even if those mathematical checks pass.
3. Expose collision witnesses/manifold data through full-hull fixed and dynamic queries. Verify face/edge/vertex contacts, pair-order invariance, touching/separating support preservation and no future-contact support latch. Native manifold aggregation and interest-notification scheduling remain separate blockers.
4. Integrate orientation, full rotated geometry, impulses and snapshots together, with identical client/server solvers and unchanged source schedule/coefficient/damping rules. Verify rotational sweeps do not tunnel or discard more integration time. Exact native rotational CCD/integrator behavior remains unproved.
5. Validate source rendering pivot and authority round-trip/late-join/replay deterministically, then run actual barrel/projectile/jump browser cases. Keep the strict jump criterion and archived failures. Improvements in off-center response cannot by themselves prove historical jump height or full V10/G06.

This work can begin with explicit mathematical adapters and exact state/transport tests, but native-fidelity acceptance needs the unresolved primary evidence above. No solver choice or native-equivalence claim is made by this audit.
