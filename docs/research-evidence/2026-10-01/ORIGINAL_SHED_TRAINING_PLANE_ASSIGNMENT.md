# Shed texture grouping without a prior camera

The globally ambiguous 11-second shed texture matches can be narrowed by treating a coherent atlas-to-image homography as a **training-only candidate for one source plane**. This is a research hypothesis, not an accepted camera or a completed interior comparison.

The [generator](../../../scripts/research/audit-shed-training-homographies.py) reads only the original decoded mesh, atlas/reference hashes and the [existing descriptor audit](woodland-11s-shed-texture-ambiguity.json). It does not read a prior camera, render a scene or execute the original game. The preassigned split is retained: 94 training matches and 61 held-out matches.

With a fixed seed of 1701, a 2-pixel RANSAC threshold, 10,000 maximum iterations, confidence 0.999 and a minimum group of 12 training matches, it extracts one group of **72 training matches** (66 distinct rounded atlas coordinates). Refitting that group with training samples alone gives **0.840979-pixel RMS**. Intersecting every member's decoded source-plane candidates leaves precisely one plane: local **X = -1352.16796875**, faces **32, 51, 74, 109 and 129**. Each training match has exactly one containing triangle on this plane. Its source bounds are X = -1352.16796875, Y = 8733.8583984375–8977.166015625, Z = 872.546875–1110.260009765625.

This resolves the plane ambiguity conditionally on the group's being one actual textured plane. A homography alone cannot establish that condition, since repeated patterns and false descriptor matches can also create a group. Source planes are grouped with normal tolerance 1e-5 and offset tolerance 0.02 source units; this preserves the approximately four-unit separation between parallel wall surfaces while tolerating decoded floating-point noise.

The held-out evidence **does not validate the whole correspondence set**. Of 61 held-out matches, 59 have exactly one candidate on the selected plane. All 59 are retained in the [candidate camera input](woodland-11s-shed-training-plane-camera-candidate.json), without pruning by homography residual. Their RMS is **171.592306 pixels**, maximum **636.594612 pixels**. Forty-eight are within the predetermined 2-pixel allowance; eleven fail, including six gross mismatches of approximately 498–637 pixels. Two remaining held-out matches do not contain this plane and are reported in the complete audit rather than silently converted into landmarks.

The [complete JSON](woodland-11s-shed-training-homographies.json) preserves every held-out error for the group, the remaining 22 training matches, source-plane definitions, hashes and method parameters. The [embedded-image SVG](woodland-11s-shed-training-homographies.svg) shows training members as cyan circles and all held-out matches as orange crosses. Candidate correspondences are explicitly marked unvalidated; the main audit's accepted `landmarks` array remains empty. Training-only RANSAC selection is disclosed and never applied to held-out samples.

Two exact reruns reproduced all three artifact bytes. No game files, GPU/browser state, original runtime or camera pose were accessed. A subsequent camera fit may use this hypothesis, but must retain the failed held-out results and cannot claim independent camera validation by selecting the 48 small residuals afterward. Independently established room/wall visibility, or a predetermined image-region assignment supported by geometry, is still needed to distinguish repeated-pattern observations without selecting them by fit error.

The root's subsequent [upright source-FOV50 fit](../../../artifacts/fidelity-review/2026-10-01/current/woodland-11s-shed-training-plane-camera-fit.json) uses all 72 candidate training points and retains all 59 compatible held-out points. Training RMS is 0.909683 pixels; held-out RMS is 171.712703 pixels and maximum 637.230118 pixels. This confirms that fitting a camera does not resolve the contradictory correspondences. The planar training group also supplies no independent depth validation. No browser capture or gameplay camera change follows from this rejected candidate.
