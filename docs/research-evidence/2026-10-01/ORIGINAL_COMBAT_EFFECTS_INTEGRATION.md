## Installed behavior

`shared/originalCombatEffects.ts` retains raw source state, including terminal updates before actor removal. `src/originalCombatEffectPresentation.ts` renders the exact texture files on independently owned materials/transforms, using the source plane size normalized by1.72/85. Source miss/blood routes replace generated impact particles, lights and marks. Source shots have no authored tracers. SPAS projects two coincident local blood/miss effects from a shared query; this does not execute two native queries. The [2026-10-06 muzzle correction](../../../artifacts/fidelity-review/2026-10-06/source-local-bullet-muzzles/EXECUTION_REPORT.md) additionally preserves two distinct local muzzle leases. The subsequent [bullet presentation correction](../../../artifacts/fidelity-review/2026-10-06/source-local-bullet-order/EXECUTION_REPORT.md) emits each effect before its muzzle using saved gun WORLD. The combined numerical damage/query adapter, complete scene membership and native actor/receipt chronology remain unresolved.

Actual local hitscan queries create blood at the source offset when the rendered actor is first hit, including online prediction. Authority damage no longer adds another local blood effect. Received remote firing does not invent a tracer or local hit query: the preserved baddies.shoot handler presents fire/muzzle, while local weaponry.bullettest owns miss/blood. Solo RPG actors create one puff on every observed callback before detonation; RG6 creates none.

The [2026-10-06 ownership correction](../../../artifacts/fidelity-review/2026-10-06/source-local-hit-ownership/EXECUTION_REPORT.md) requires the target mesh to belong to its currently registered local/remote actor before blood is selected. Anonymous/stale bindings produce miss/muzzle without damage reporting and still reach any-hit bookkeeping. Pending-death receiver membership remains eligible; native complete scene queries remain unproved.

Online prediction retains a callback cursor across authority adoption and repeated render evaluation. Known skipped callback positions replay through the shared geometry adapter, with individual puff ages restored. A predicted terminal puff deduplicates its later authority explosion, including explosion before projectile adoption. An older active authority checkpoint does not disprove a later predicted terminal callback; a checkpoint which survived that callback does re-arm future effects. Late joins lack earlier trajectory history and begin presenting future callbacks rather than fabricating historical puffs. Session/map cleanup removes all actors and per-instance textures/materials. Per-instance birth/restoration epochs skip older or duplicate catch-up callbacks before aging an actor; untimed staging retains one-call/one-callback behavior. These guards also protect late-join explosion atlases.
