# Stone-building yard and exposed bank

The [decoded Woodland frame at 15 seconds](woodland-video-15s.jpg), from the preserved [silent first-45-second excerpt](woodland-video-first45s.mp4), shows a low stone facade with a dark open bay, adjacent closed plank doors and a tall brown mottled bank behind its right-hand roof. The front yard reads as low ground. See [source provenance](source-provenance.json) for the recording and extraction history.

The copy's previous outbuilding view looked downward from terrain around 7.98m while the building floor was around 3.59m. Its smooth, low grassy backdrop did not reproduce the visible bank. This supports reshaping the broad yard/bank composition, but provides no original coordinates or dimensions.

The authored correction keeps the building floor and existing doorway terraces level, adds a low front yard centred at (33,-18), and raises a continuous bank centred around (41,-41). The bank starts beyond the rear terrace at z=-36.5 and has up to 16.5m of added elevation. Shared client/server ground support, trunk placement heights and terrain combat queries all use the same height function. Steep faces use mottled stone with a brown palette rather than separate floating rock props. The terrain remains one shared height mesh with two material batches. These values are authoring choices guided by screen composition, not historical measurements.

The first render exposed too little bank above the roof and looked through the front bay into a solid rear wall wing. Moving the crest closer/raising it and centring the authored review camera on the bay corrects those comparison problems. The station remains an authored view, not a recovered original camera transform. The copy retains its own building dimensions, roof form and route inventory.

Focused tests verify level entrances, actual client climbing, gravity-driven descent/landing and bank combat hits. The final bank also crosses a horizontal 16m sightline above the former 14.1m raycast bound; synchronising the analytic bound prevents skipping that visible terrain. Low/high rendered views show the bank above the right roof and an open bay. A later surface review replaces the flattened yard's green coverage with local dark earth and the vertically stretched fine grain with a coarse angular brown/gray bank texture. The bank uses projected world X/Y UVs on its visible steep face while ordinary ground retains horizontal UVs; no terrain or collision changes accompany that surface pass. Internal tests establish shared behavior, not original topology or numerical gameplay parity. Exact rock pattern, exposure and bank contour remain unresolved.

A later [asymmetric-facade pass](OUTBUILDING_OBSERVATIONS.md) moves the bay into the short left section, splits the roof and closes the previously authored opposed rear opening. The historical rear remains unverified; current movement now enters and returns through the front instead of crossing the building. Earlier through-route checks above describe the prior implementation, not the current route. Terrain and yard are unchanged by that pass.

A further sharp-frame review found that the first angular surface formed a regular mosaic of individually outlined small stones, unlike the source's broad fractured rock mixed with earth. The current texture uses fewer overlapping skewed slabs, partial low-contrast fissures and wrapped soft earth stains at two scales. Low/high outbuilding views were inspected after this correction. The faces are still authored, and their repetition, relief, original contour and camera registration are not recovered; V06 remains open. Terrain, UV projection, collision and shared support are unchanged.
