Russias Army, Comrades in Arms (browser 3D Multiplayer First Person Shooter game)

Russias Army game, meet people from all over the world... and shoot them. Welcome to Gamevial's Russias Army, Massive Multiplayer Online Game, MMOG, an online multiplayer first person shooter set in the cold war. Tell your friends, meet them, chat, and kill each other all in glorious and harmless Shockwave 3D. ARROW keys or 'W' 'A' 'S' and 'D' strafing controls to move, MOUSE to steer, . 'TAB' and 'ENTER' to chat. LEFT MOUSE to shoot. 1-6 weapon select, SHIFT to zoom sniper rifle. Note: this is a beta work in progress, please expect bugs, and bear with us... Visit our official Russias Army game website at www.russiasarmy.com for more game news and updates.
