# Preserved numerical hitscan rules

Static decompilation only; the Director VM was not executed. Evidence paths below are relative to `director-script-extraction/` in this directory.

Both editions define pistol damage12, SPAS12 damage18 per ray, AK47 damage6, and Dragunov damage36. Barrack `casts/parents/ParentScript 6 - weaponry.ls:216–245` and Woodland equivalent lines209–238 compute every ray from `cosine3D(goody.rot)`. Neither uses hit location to multiply damage. `MovieScript 1 - generics.ls:51–59` implements the deterministic camera direction.

SPAS invokes bullettest twice with coordinates310,190 and330,210 (Barrack181–185; Woodland170–174), but the bullettest coords argument is unused. Both rays therefore coincide; their damage accumulates to36 for the same target. `ParentScript 8 - baddies.ls:127–129` only plays a hurt sound locally, so the first ray does not kill or move the target before the second. The authoritative adapter consumes one chamber round and one registered hit, applying36 once rather than authorizing two independently replayable claims.

Woodland gun decrements waitgun before its readiness check and assigns8/15/2/50 after firing (weaponry136,154–192). Barrack tests the previously calculated delta before incrementing its counter, then resets waitgun to poldwaitgun on firing (weaponry156–175). Starting immediately after a shot with delta0, a wait ofN reaches old deltaN on callbackN+1. Barrack's repeating intervals are therefore9/16/3/51. These intervals describe repeated shots of one weapon; first-shot, reload and cross-weapon readiness depend on the shared raw counter state.

The movie declares30fps; dividing callbacks by30 is a nominal scheduling adaptation, not proof of measured playback speed. Recoil follows ray resolution: common pitch+1, SPAS additional+6, Drag additional+2, yaw `(random(3)-2)*0.5` degrees (Barrack186–203; Woodland175–196). Random3 yields1..3, hence discrete yaw−0.5/0/+0.5degrees. There is no per-ray random spread or aiming/sprinting spread multiplier here.
