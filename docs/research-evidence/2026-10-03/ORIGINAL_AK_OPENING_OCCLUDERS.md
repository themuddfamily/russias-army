# AK sight opening against all source components

The new [all-component line inventory](ak-opening-occluders/all-component-opening-lines.json), generated by [this passive script](../../../scripts/research/audit-original-ak-opening-occluders.py), checks every one of the 359 AK triangles against the central source-X line from the [manifold audit](ORIGINAL_AK_SIGHT_MANIFOLD.md). Exact triangle/vertex IDs, positions, raw UVs and both possible X depth orders are retained. The seven component definitions come from the hashed exact-coordinate topology graph and are numerical component IDs, not authored named parts.

The sight component alone cleared this line, but the **whole AK mesh does not**. Six nondegenerate finite triangle intersections occur, all outside the sight component:

All six hit points share the declared source Y≈0.0000000298 and Z≈6.5869727135 and retain finite triangle barycentrics. The projected minimum clearance over the whole mesh is zero. Twelve triangles from other components overlap the projected candidate disk with radius 0.2349715233 source units. This invalidates treating the earlier component-local clearance as an unobstructed whole-weapon corridor. It does not prove which triangle occludes a rendered aperture: an eye position, perspective direction, culling and near clipping are not established.

Source-X parallel probes through the three crown coordinate groups 42,44,153 each retain four nondegenerate intersections, all within the sight component. No other-component nondegenerate point hit was found for these tip lines. This is a conditional direction diagnostic, not independent image-tip identity. Projected-degenerate triangle cases retain their status and segment-distance clearance diagnostics in the central-line inventory; missing nondegenerate point hits alone are not a proof against coplanar contact.

The copy's ideal quarter-turn mapping [sourceY,sourceZ,sourceX] would put source X along camera-local depth before the unknown runtime translation. The audit retains both X orderings and does not choose which component is between a finite eye and the sight. Both aspect hypotheses remain unchanged; native Euler, bob/recoil/loading phase, near clipping, crop and actual silhouette are unresolved. Parallel source-axis probes are not a fitted perspective camera.

No accepted, fit or heldout image observations are created. A next independent source-only check could evaluate full-mesh projected-degenerate contacts and compare finite rim/tip geometry with preserved imagery. Any perspective visibility test would require a declared conditional eye/translation and keep native pose uncertainty; an accepted camera fit still requires independently identified image features and separate validation. No browser, renderer or original runtime was run. Production, progress and previous evidence are unchanged; V23 remains open.
