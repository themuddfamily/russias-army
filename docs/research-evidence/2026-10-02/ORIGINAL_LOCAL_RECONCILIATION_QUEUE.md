# Atomic source player/barrel reconciliation

The source online client previously replayed every received physical correction before handling the next snapshot. Opt-in stage measurements in the unchanged Barrack barrel case showed up to206.2ms in one replay, while authority receipts grew stale. This measurement concerns the reconstructed browser client, not Director/Havok timing.

Negotiated source online sessions now retain a detached newest eligible player correction and its matching barrel packet. Snapshot receipts, health/phase updates, remote actors, projectiles, blasts and events are still processed in order. Player/barrel bodies and their rendered pivots stay together at the previously applied state until a physical or spatial consumer commits the pair. Commit happens before source physics, rendering, firing, movement publication, pickup reach and camera-dependent audio. Spawn/death, map, round, transport and fixture transitions invalidate obsolete queued work. Ordinary compatibility sessions retain immediate reconciliation.

Received barrel time, pending correction time and applied physical time are distinct observations. Debug reads never flush the queue. An unmatched newer barrel packet first consumes a valid pending player/barrel correction. Contact coverage uses the applied or pending local correction epoch rather than a newer room timestamp; otherwise a contact could disappear without an applied body accounting for it. Older canonical packets cannot roll the bodies back. The queue does not advance gun/projectile counters or invent acknowledgements.

Twenty actual Game tests cover ordered immediate effects, detached inputs, newest-epoch selection, exact recovered Barrack body/quaternion/velocity/model parity, cancellation, debug coherence and contact coverage. In the final five-case browser run, online Barrack barrel passes every existing assertion with zero sampled penetration and no server errors. Both authoritative grenade cases and Woodland solo grenade still fail unchanged real-time checks.
