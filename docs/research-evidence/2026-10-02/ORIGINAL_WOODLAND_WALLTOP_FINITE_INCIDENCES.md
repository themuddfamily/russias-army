# Woodland compatible interior wall-top finite incidences

The new [source-only finite triangle inventory](interior-walltop-incidences/finite-walltop-incidences.json), generated by [this script](../../../scripts/research/inventory-original-shed-walltop-incidences.py), examines compatible wall planes A8/12/25 and B10/14/26 against every same-component sloped-normal triangle. The upper half of the compatible walls' source Z extent is an explicit exploratory wall-top filter. Sloped orientation alone does not establish roof semantics. All 196 original faces, 415 exact positions and first-channel UVs are preserved, including exactly degenerate face 62.

After finite bounding-box screening, 126 wall/sloped triangle pairs receive vertex-in-triangle and edge/plane intersection checks. Vertices must lie near the opposite plane and within the opposite triangle; exact edge/plane crossings must lie within that finite triangle. Admission uses physical oriented edge-halfspace distances at 0.00001, 0.0001, 0.001 and 0.002 source-unit allowances. Every candidate retains exact triangle coordinates and indices, source plane IDs, derived incidence endpoint coordinates, plane-distance diagnostics, barycentrics and UVs from both faces. Derived endpoints are diagnostics; they do not alter decoded geometry or become accepted image landmarks.

All four allowances retain the same counts: 16 A candidate pairs and 17 B pairs. Four A pairs and two B pairs have finite incidence length above 0.002 source units at the strictest allowance. The remaining 12 A and 15 B pairs are point contacts or numerical endpoint slivers, retained without promotion to finite boundary support.

Each of these six substantial incidences already shares a full finite edge group in the earlier [junction inventory](ORIGINAL_WOODLAND_SOURCE_JUNCTION_INVENTORY.md). This triangle-interior follow-up therefore adds no new substantial wall-top roof/wall boundary and does not resolve the asymmetric candidate count. The result concerns this explicit finite triangle subset and allowance range; it is not proof of absent roof geometry, a geometric hole, native mesh connectivity or component identity.

Nonzero allowances can admit nearby disconnected triangles and slightly extrapolated UVs. Coplanar pairs and general intersections outside the chosen wall-top region are outside this pass. The original source coordinates remain authoritative; numerical diagnostics are never substituted for them.
