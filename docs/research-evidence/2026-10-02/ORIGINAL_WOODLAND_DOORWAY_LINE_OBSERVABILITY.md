# 13s doorway lines: source hypotheses and observability

This pass revalidates the [13s boundary inventory](ORIGINAL_WOODLAND_13S_BOUNDARY_INVENTORY.md) and [independent tank correspondence](ORIGINAL_WOODLAND_13S_TANK_CORRESPONDENCE.md), then asks whether the two actual doorway-line candidates plus one ambiguous tank feature can identify a camera without claiming line-sample endpoints are source vertices.

The [source-only enumeration/rank script](../../../scripts/research/audit-reference-doorway-line-rank.py) retains **64 hypotheses**: both shed components, every opposite-side inner/outer jamb combination, both image-left/right assignments and all four source alternatives for the measured tank feature. Opposite-side pairs are selected only by the source horizontal doorway gap90..110; all eight source jamb edges and tank faces30,42,1280,1284 participate. No historical residual ranks or selects labels. The tank's preassigned `fit` role remains unchanged; none of the seven texture matches becomes a heldout point.

The [complete rank/input record](interior-13s-line-rank/source-doorway-line-rank.json) stores exact source edges, image-line coefficients, measured tank pixels, all source alternatives and consistent synthetic projections. Infinite-line incidence uses two projected source endpoints to test whether a3D line lies on an observed image line; it does not identify either projected endpoint with the photographed segment samples. Source vertical FOV50 and centered recording principal point are explicit retained assumptions; horizontal recording stretch remains free.

For ideal parallel vertical3D lines and an upright camera, the shared vertical vanishing point has image x equal to the centered principal point. Two image lines therefore supply three independent quantities, rather than four. The tank point adds two, giving five independent observations for six unknowns: yaw, pitch, three position coordinates and horizontal stretch. With camera bank free, there are at most six observations for seven unknowns. Neither model establishes a unique camera from this set alone.

Finite differences of consistent synthetic projections verify the ideal bounds for every hypothesis: upright rank5of6 and free-bank rank6of7. Synthetic data prevents incompatible/noisy historical line measurements from supplying spurious rank. These are observability checks, not fitted or likely historical poses, and they do not provide independent heldout validation. The [summary](interior-13s-line-rank/rank-summary.json) retains the numerical spectra and threshold sensitivity.

Exact source endpoints require an additional qualification. A few decoded jamb edges have tiny horizontal endpoint offsets, up to0.000019761world units. The script preserves their coordinates unchanged and checks this exact model separately from the explicitly ideal parallel-line diagnostic. These tiny departures technically supply weak extra rank, so the exact source cannot simply be called perfectly parallel or silently rounded into that model. Their image sensitivity at the recorded synthetic poses is far below the retained two-pixel measurement allowance; the summary reports the exact maximum. This is a local diagnostic, not a universal bound at the unknown historical pose.
