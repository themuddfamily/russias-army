# Original Barrack and Woodland visual research

Researched 2026-09-29. This is a correction backlog, not a claim that changes have been implemented. **[ ] means still to correct or investigate; confidence labels distinguish visible evidence from unresolved details.** Target versions are the original Barrack and Woodland, not Next Gen.

## Evidence and limits

The strongest evidence here is **actual gameplay imagery inspected from YouTube storyboard sheets**, not search descriptions. Full-video download returned HTTP 403 for the 2010 recording, so motion, precise timings, animation cadence, and tiny HUD text were not verified. Storyboards are low-resolution contact sheets; coordinates below use one-based row/column. Approximate timestamps are navigation aids computed from storyboard cadence, not frame-accurate observations.

## Barrack correction checklist

- [ ] **V-B01 — Replace the winter ground palette. High confidence.** B10 sheet 0 rows 1–2 and B12 sheet 0 show extensive green grass surrounding gray concrete. The copy instead authors packed snow, icy asphalt, snow drifts, tracks and snowfall (`src/arena.ts:754`, `src/game.ts:568`). For a Barrack look, establish green grass / worn concrete first; snow is not supported by these recordings.
- [ ] **V-B02 — Rebuild the map's dominant massing. High confidence for shape; layout still unmeasured.** B10 sheet 0 rows 3–5 shows broad flat pale rooftops, parapets, long elevated walkways and repeated ramps between levels. The copy is four freestanding buildings around a road cross (`src/arena.ts:830`–833). Capture and reconstruct the original connected roof/ramp silhouette before adding detail. Do not derive exact topology or dimensions from these tiny views.
- [ ] **V-B03 — Restore sloping concrete routes as major visible geometry. High confidence.** B12 sheet 1 rows 1–3 repeatedly shows long triangular side profiles and ramps climbing toward roof levels. Copy geometry and collision need a dedicated route comparison, not merely decorative roof trim. Acceptance: matching reference viewpoints show the same main rising paths and parapet lines.
- [ ] **V-B04 — Match building facade families. High confidence.** B10 sheet 0 bottom-right and B12 sheet 0 bottom row show weathered brown brick, red lower panels/sections, tall pale window frames and a projecting entrance canopy. Copy has original Cyrillic-signposted concrete, red metal and corrugated warehouse buildings (`src/arena.ts:830`–833). Use these recorded facade proportions/material blocks, then settle exact window count from better frames.
- [ ] **V-B05 — Restore the long low horizon and readable cloudy sky. High confidence.** B12 presents a dark tree line under a gray-blue sky with a bright textured cloud band. Copy's fog-matched white-out winter atmosphere and pine perimeter have a different silhouette. Compare cloud band, horizon height and visibility at matched camera positions; do not substitute a generic snowy overcast.
- [ ] **V-B06 — Match vegetation distribution. Medium confidence.** B10 shows occasional isolated trees near grassy outer areas and a distant forest line, not a uniform ring of stylized winter pines. Audit `src/instancedFoliage.ts` and `src/winterBackdrop.ts` after map massing is fixed. Tree species cannot be reliably identified from these sheets.
- [ ] **V-B07 — Rebalance surface lighting toward the recorded flat, worn appearance. Medium confidence.** Concrete and weapons in B10/B12 read as low-resolution textured polygon surfaces with relatively simple shading. Copy deliberately uses procedural PBR materials, modern shadows and effects. Preserve original broad colors and contrast at gameplay distance; pixel-for-pixel shader fidelity is not inferred from compressed recordings.

## Woodland correction checklist

- [ ] **V-W01 — Add an actual Woodland scene. High confidence.** W14 is a distinct hilly forest/countryside environment. Copy has one flat winter Barrack-inspired arena; perimeter trees do not reproduce Woodland. Keep Barrack and Woodland as separate reference sets and eventual maps.
- [ ] **V-W02 — Model substantial grassy hills and valleys. High confidence.** W14 sheet 0 bottom rows and sheet 1 rows 1–2 show rolling green hills, pronounced rises, and low dark terrain between them. Current snow relief is presentation-only (`src/arena.ts` ArenaData comment) and does not provide these traversable height changes. Acceptance requires matching hill silhouettes and elevation-dependent views.
- [ ] **V-W03 — Bring trees into playable space. High confidence.** W14 sheet 0 row 2 and sheet 2 rows 1–4 show the camera among trunks and dense foliage, not outside a decorative treeline. Match thin, light-toned trunks and irregular leafy silhouettes. Exact species and collision behavior remain unresolved.
- [ ] **V-W04 — Add rough dark earth and rocky slope zones. High confidence.** W14 sheet 1 rows 2–4 and sheet 2 show steep brown/rocky faces and shaded gullies beside green ground. Copy's snow/icy-road materials and flat arena cannot convey this landform contrast. Prioritize terrain color zoning and cliff silhouettes over fine material detail.
- [ ] **V-W05 — Replace compound-scale buildings with scattered rural structures. High confidence.** W14 sheet 0 rows 1, 3–5 and sheet 1 row 1 show small worn light-plaster buildings, dark timber sheds, low pitched roofs and a larger weathered two-story structure. Copy has warehouse/barracks/medpoint military compound forms. Record each reference structure separately before creating an asset list; exact count is not established.
- [ ] **V-W06 — Match sparse rustic interiors. High confidence for sampled rooms.** W14 sheet 0 row 3 columns 3–4 and row 4 column 3 show bare rooms, pale small-paned windows and plank floors. Current lit furnished warehouse/barracks/medpoint interiors have a different character (`src/arenaInteriors.ts`). Do not fill original-looking rooms with unsupported modern military furniture.
- [ ] **V-W07 — Reconstruct fences in relation to terrain. High confidence for presence.** W14 sheet 2 row 3 column 1 clearly includes chain-link fencing on a slope; other views show fence lines along elevated boundaries. Copy also has chain-link infrastructure, so the difference is layout, scale and relation to hills, not absence of the material.
- [ ] **V-W08 — Match the broad daylight palette. High confidence.** W14 combines green grass, dark earth, gray-blue sky and dark green tree crowns. Copy is predominantly white/gray winter terrain. Use fixed comparison views in both sunlit/open grass and dark wooded gullies so exposure does not erase their contrast.

## Shared UI, first-person presentation and character checklist

- [ ] **V-U01 — Recreate the original menu composition. High confidence, Barrack evidence.** B10 sheet 0 tiles (1,1), (1,2), (2,4), (3,1) shows angular red/black/white graphics, a large white circular panel at left, and logo/emblem on the right. Current menu is a left-aligned modern typographic stack over a generated scenic background (`src/style.css:16`, `index.html:15`). Woodland menu was not captured in W14; do not assume identical without another source.
- [ ] **V-U02 — Restore visible room-selection and death-screen styling. High confidence, Barrack evidence.** B10 shows a room list inside that white disk and a death page using the same graphic treatment. Copy's room-code field and dark mission-report panel have different visual hierarchy. Fine text and precise row dimensions require larger captures.
- [ ] **V-U03 — Move the ammunition overview into the top-left. High confidence.** B10/B12/W14 consistently show a compact multirow colored weapon/ammo table top-left; copy shows only current weapon/ammo bottom-right (`index.html:71`). Confirm exact row order/labels from larger frames before implementing it.
- [ ] **V-U04 — Restore the top-right health display. High confidence for location and red-cross symbol.** References show a prominent red cross top-right; copy uses bottom-left health and armor bars (`index.html:67`). Exact numerals and whether other status fields change by version remain to transcribe.
- [ ] **V-U05 — Restore lower-left compact chat/log text. High confidence.** Reference sheets show several green/yellow text lines directly over gameplay in the lower-left. Copy occupies that corner with status bars and lacks chat UI. This visual difference exists independently of whether chat networking is later implemented.
- [ ] **V-U06 — Match lower-right emblem treatment. High confidence.** B10/B12/W14 show a conspicuous gold/yellow hammer-and-sickle-like emblem and small red/white wordmark bottom-right. Current lower-right space is a large weapon/ammunition panel. Trace layout from better source artwork; do not claim the small wordmark is legible in these sheets.
- [ ] **V-U07 — Remove or separately toggle unmatched HUD overlays for fidelity comparison. High confidence for the sampled views only.** Current top mission name, first-to-12 banner, live leader strip, radar and modern panel system (`index.html:53`–77) are absent from the sampled original views. This is not proof no original build ever had such elements; compare against the chosen desktop versions.
- [ ] **V-U08 — Match scope masking and scale. Medium confidence.** B12 shows a circular scope window on a black surround with HUD corners retained. Copy has its own 4×24 overlay (`index.html:61`). Measure aperture size, reticle and vignette from a larger frame before changing; presence of a scope alone is already shared.
- [ ] **V-U09 — Match first-person weapon silhouettes, color and occupied screen area. Medium confidence.** References show compact angular gray/black weapons, including blue-gray tubular/receiver forms and wood-colored rifle sections. Copy uses detailed modern procedural models and upgraded variants. Capture one clear hip-fire and aimed frame per weapon; tiny sheets do not establish exact models, FOV or animation timing.
- [ ] **V-U10 — Audit soldier kit against close frames before redesigning. Unresolved.** B10/B12 contain small visible soldiers but insufficient detail here to establish original helmet, face, uniform pattern or gear inventory. Copy's goggles, plate carriers, packs and pads are documented additions; their exact visual discrepancy needs better close reference. Do not invent original uniforms from modern Russian military imagery.
- [ ] **V-U11 — Separate sky fidelity from rendering quality. High confidence.** Both reference maps have low-detail but visibly differentiated sky/horizon bands. Current winter white-out has different composition even at high render quality. Better geometry/shadows alone will not correct this.
